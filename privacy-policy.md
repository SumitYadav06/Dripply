## 1. What this app is
Dripply is a free water-sort puzzle game with 60 handcrafted levels, extra levels loaded from the web, a daily challenge puzzle and endless generated levels. The game is free and earns only through ads. There is no paid Premium, no in-app purchase and no subscription in this version.

## 2. Accounts, logins and payments
The app does not ask you to create an account, log in, or give your name, email address, phone number or payment details. No payment is collected inside the app.

## 3. Data the app itself stores
Your progress (coins, stars, which levels are unlocked and completed), language choice, theme and music/sound settings are saved only on your own device. This information is not sent to us and we cannot see it. On launch the app fetches an extra-levels file from a public web address; that request does not include any personal information.

## 4. Advertising — Google AdMob
The app shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on the Home screen only,
a video interstitial after every 3rd completed level, an app-open ad when
the app opens, and rewarded video ads for hints, extra tubes, extra undos,
free coins and doubling the daily reward (only when the player taps Watch Ad). AdMob and Google may collect and use device information to show ads, for example:
- device identifiers (such as the advertising ID),
- approximate location (derived from IP address),
- app usage and ad interaction data,
- device and network information.

This data is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
You can reset or limit ad personalisation from your device settings (Google Settings > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads on your device). The app does not control which specific ad is shown to you.

## 5. Children
The app is a general-audience puzzle game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has shared personal information with us, contact us at the email above and we will help remove it. Parents can also use their device's parental controls to manage ads and app use.

## 6. Third parties
Apart from Google AdMob for advertising, the app does not share information with other third parties, and does not use analytics that identify you personally.

## 7. Your choices
- You can stop using the app at any time; deleting the app also deletes the progress saved on your device.
- You can reset your advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in your device settings, as described in section 4.

## 8. Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same link, with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
